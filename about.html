About

Hi, I'm Judy.

I started in software and found my way to medicine. Today I work where the two meet.

I studied computer science at Michigan State and spent nearly two years as a software engineer at Target, building backend services and data pipelines with Kotlin, Kafka and PostgreSQL. I loved the craft, but I kept being drawn to problems in health.

So I made the jump. At Michigan Medicine I now support research in digital pathology, AI and cancer diagnostics: cleaning and analyzing clinical datasets in Python, co-authoring papers, writing grants, and coordinating a global pathology consortium with investigators in more than 25 countries. I also work as a medical assistant, which keeps me close to patients.

I'm most interested in how careful engineering and good data can make medicine better, and I'm continuing to build toward a career at that intersection.

Outside of work I train for marathons, box, practice yoga, shoot film and digital photography, and travel whenever I can.
